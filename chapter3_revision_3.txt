  CLRerNet on CULane, and YOLOPX and HybridNets reaching their highest recall on
  BDD100K - and CLRerNet's precision advantage is consistent across TuSimple,
  CULane, and CurveLanes."

C-2. Methodology gap (printed p.19-20, "Core Evaluation Metrics"). The results
  prose says "once the rasterization width and tolerance are applied consistently
  (see methodology)" but the methodology never defines them. Your comment GB40
  flags the same gap. ADD to the end of "Core Evaluation Metrics" (use your real
  width/tolerance, e.g. 16 px / 8 px):
     "Because lane-line detectors such as CLRerNet output structured polylines
     rather than dense masks, each model's lane output is rendered to a binary
     lane mask at a fixed stroke width of W px, and a stroke-width tolerance of
     T px is applied symmetrically to both the predicted and reference masks
     before pixel-level true positives, false positives, and false negatives are
     counted. The same width and tolerance are applied identically to all four
     models so that thin-line and dense-mask outputs are compared on geometric
     agreement rather than raster width. Even so, a thin-line detector is expected
     to score lower on pixel IoU/F1 than a dense-mask model, which is why per-
     dataset precision and recall are also reported."
  CONFIRM the tolerance was applied to ALL four models, not only CLRerNet.
